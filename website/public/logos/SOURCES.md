# Logo artwork

These marks identify calendar providers and browsers; they do not imply endorsement.

- iCloud: Apple logo artwork from https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:ICloud_logo.svg (public-domain simple logo).
- Google: `google-icon` from `@iconify-json/logos` 1.2.15, sourced from https://github.com/gilbarbara/logos (CC0).
- Microsoft Exchange: Microsoft Office team artwork from https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Microsoft_Exchange_(2019-present).svg (public-domain simple geometry).

Trademarks belong to their respective owners. UI icons are Hugeicons Stroke Rounded from `@hugeicons/core-free-icons` 4.3.5; the MIT licence is included at `/licenses/Hugeicons-MIT.txt`.

Safari, Chrome, Firefox and Microsoft Edge: SVG artwork from the CC0 [gilbarbara/logos collection](https://github.com/gilbarbara/logos), files `safari.svg`, `chrome.svg`, `firefox.svg` and `microsoft-edge.svg`.
