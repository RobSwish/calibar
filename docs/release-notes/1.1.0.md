# CaliBar 1.1.0

Create appointments directly from the menu bar. Click the new + button or press Command-N, choose a calendar, and set the title, location or meeting link, dates, time zone, repeats and alerts. Press Command-Return to save.

- Swipe horizontally on your trackpad to move between calendar months.
- Hold Option over the menu-bar item to reveal Join for its video meeting, then click to join. Command-click still works.
- Hover over an invitee to reveal an email button.
- Glass dropdowns with calendar colours, compact alert controls, and clearer date fields.
- Improved panel transitions, footer alignment and dialog clipping; removed the duplicate Open Apple Calendar menu command.

For invitations, attachments, travel time and FaceTime-link creation, use Save and open in Apple Calendar. Calendar accounts remain managed by macOS.

Free. Requires macOS 14 or later. Supports Apple silicon and Intel. Developer ID signed, notarized by Apple, and available through CaliBar’s built-in update checker.
